This page will cover node shares, which are a different axis from subshell shares. The owner grants view or edit on a node; the asymmetry that surprises people is that ANY node share — even view — lets the grantee launch their own subshells on that machine, and what they launch stays private to them unless they share it. Edit adds configuring (re-checks); shares, rename, stop, uninstall and repointing stay owner-only. The page must open with the consequence: a node share extends your machine's command surface to another user's workloads, and only the machine's owner can see what invisible work is running on it.
