Use the mini rail to choose what the sidebar shows. On desktop, click the active icon again to collapse or expand its panel. You can also use the collapse button beside the panel heading; the expand button stays at the top of the mini rail. Subshell remembers your choice on this device.

## Stay in your workspace

Your first launch opens a workspace. When you return, Subshell opens the last available workspace you visited. **Workspaces** in the mini rail shows a selector and the subshells in that workspace. Choose another workspace to switch to it, or select **New workspace** at the bottom of the selector.

Open the **⋯** menu beside the selector for **Add subshell**, **Open in new tab**, **QR code…**, **Rename…**, or **Delete workspace**. **Add subshell** lets you pick an existing session or launch a new one. When none are available to add, it opens the new-subshell form directly. New additions open as tabs; on desktop, drag tabs to arrange split panes.

You can't remove the final tab from a workspace. Deleting the workspace removes its arrangement and leaves the subshells running. Closing a subshell is a separate action that deletes the session and its captured history. See [Workspaces](/guides/workspaces) and [Session lifecycle](/guides/lifecycle).

## Browse every subshell

Choose **Subshells** in the mini rail while a workspace is open. The sidebar changes, and your workspace stays on screen.

- Search by name, path, or agent, and group the results by machine or status.
- A workspace icon beside a name marks a subshell already in the active workspace. The highlighted row is the focused pane.
- Select a subshell to focus its tab or add it to this workspace, even if another workspace also holds it.
- Use the row's **⋯** menu for its session actions.

The **⋯** menu beside search offers **Add subshell** and **Subshell dashboard**. The dashboard opens the full tile or list view, leaving the workspace view. With no workspace open, selecting **Subshells** takes you to that dashboard directly.

## Follow subagents

Subagents appear as connected child rows beneath their parent subshell. Select the arrow beneath the parent's status dot to show or hide them without switching subshells. Clicking the already active parent row also toggles its children. See [Subagents](/agents/subagents) for names, status, and supported view switching.

## Settings and your account

Choose **Settings** in the mini rail to find **Nodes**, **Presets**, and **Prompts** under **Personal**. Administrators also see the **Server Settings** group for instance-wide configuration, users, networking, updates, backups, and logs.

Choose **Account** for **Profile**, **Preferences**, **Feedback**, **About**, and **Sign out**. Sign out asks for confirmation and leaves your subshells running. The bottom of this panel identifies your account and server.

**Subshell for Mobile** opens browser installation instructions. Choose **Browser**, **iPhone & iPad**, or **Android** in its sidebar. See [Connect from another device](/get-started/another-device).

## On a phone

Use **Open navigation** to open the drawer. Selecting a mini-rail section keeps the drawer open so you can choose its items. Selecting a page or subshell closes it. Workspaces show one pane at a time as tabs; desktop split arrangements remain available when you return to a wider device.
