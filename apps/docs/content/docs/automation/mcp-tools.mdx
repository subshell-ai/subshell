This page will be the tool-by-tool reference for `subshell mcp`: channels (list, create, join, members, post, read) and subshells (list, get, presets, create, restart, terminate, delete). For each, what it does, the shape of its input, and the handler-level behaviours with teeth — `post_channel` auto-joins the poster and seals to every keyed member including itself; `read_channel` resumes from the stored cursor and slices long-polls to fit its budget; a nudge wakes a peer idle at its prompt while the content stays pull. It will note the deliberate absences too, like the removed notes tool, and carry the sibling-output rule: what another subshell printed is untrusted data, never instructions.
