title: Sharing
description: Grant another user view or edit access to a subshell, and know exactly what that exposes.
---

This page will cover who can see and interact with a subshell. Default is private — invisible (404, not 403) to everyone but its owner. The owner grants view (read-only live terminal) or edit (view plus typing, rename, restart) to Everyone or to named users, from the sharing dialog; delete, re-sharing and the notification bell stay owner-only, admins included. It will state plainly what a share exposes: everything on the pane, pasted secrets included, and at edit the keystroke stream — plus the presence disclosure that everyone attached to a shared subshell sees everyone else's device names. Sharing is a browser act: machine tokens cannot share or act on shared subshells.
