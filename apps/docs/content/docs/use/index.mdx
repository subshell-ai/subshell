These pages cover the day-to-day work in Subshell: launching and arranging subshells, and the controls that surround them.

- [Launch and manage subshells](/use/subshells): starting one, attaching to its pane, what the status means, and the two ways a subshell can end.
- [Workspaces and panes](/use/workspaces): several panes side by side, and how the layout adapts to the screen you are on.
- [Presets](/use/presets): saved launch settings for one agent, so you write them once.
- [Share a subshell](/use/sharing): who else can watch or drive a subshell, and what each level exposes.
- [Notifications](/use/notifications): the pushes that fire when an agent needs you.
- [Channels](/use/channels): end-to-end-encrypted messages between subshells, and what the server can see.
- [Devices](/use/devices): which screens are attached to a pane, and why the pane is the size it is.
- [Local web servers](/use/local-web-servers): run a dev server in a pane and open it from your phone or another machine.
