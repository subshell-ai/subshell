Manage who can sign in to your Subshell server.

## Before you start

Sign in as an administrator in the server dashboard. Account administration isn't available through machine credentials.

## Create and review accounts

1. In the server dashboard's sidebar, open **Settings** → **Users**.
2. Select **Add user** to open the account form.
3. Review its role and status.
4. Choose the **Pending approval** tab to review accounts awaiting approval.

Creating an account administratively doesn't require enabling public registration. Users' names, email addresses, roles, and disabled states are visible through the authenticated roster used by sharing pickers.

## Roles

Members manage their own sessions and access shared resources. Administrators also manage instance settings. To view or control someone else's session, they need a sharing grant, just like any other user.

Only a session's owner can delete it, change its sharing, or control its notification bell.

## Disable access

Disabling an account prevents normal session authentication. Review any long-lived integrations separately, rather than assuming a human sign-in action revokes every kind of machine credential.
