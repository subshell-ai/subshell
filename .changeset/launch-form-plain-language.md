The launch form no longer asks for a subshell name: the server names one after its start time, the pane's own title takes over, and renaming is a deliberate act on a subshell that exists ("Edit title" in its actions menu). The setup assistant's first-launch screen also leads with plain words — **Machine** and **Agent** — and teaches "node" and "profile" in a line underneath, rather than opening with two nouns a ninety-second-old account has never met. Every other launch surface keeps the bare nouns, and the clone dialog keeps its name box, where naming the copy is the whole decision. The phone's new-subshell tab drops its name field to match.
