# Security policy

Subshell is designed to run on your own machines and private networks; its
full security model, including where it deliberately falls short, is documented
in [docs/security-overview.md](docs/security-overview.md), with the engineering
threat model in [docs/security.md](docs/security.md).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues **privately** to **theo@disaresta.com** rather
than opening a public issue. We aim to acknowledge within two business days.

We ask for no public disclosure before a fix ships or 90 days pass, whichever
comes first, and we are happy to credit responsible reports in the advisory.
